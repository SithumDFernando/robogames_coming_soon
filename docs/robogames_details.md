## 1. Event Overview

**RoboGames 2027** is Sri Lanka's premier annual robotics competition, bringing together school students, university undergraduates, hobbyists, and combat-robotics builders from across the nation. 

The competition is structured into three distinct categories spanning two simulation phases in **Cyberbotics Webots** and a grand in-person finale at the **University of Moratuwa**.

---

## 2. Competition Categories

### 2.1 School Category
*Webots simulation challenge transitioning to a physical line-following hardware final.*

* **Target Participants:** Grade 6–13 students from any recognized school in Sri Lanka.
* **Team Structure:** Teams of 2–4 members + 1 Teacher Advisor (mandatory). All members must attend the same school.
* **Competition Path:**
  * **Phase 01 (Online Qualifier):** Webots simulation qualifier.
    * *Submission Deadline:* **13 February**
  * **Phase 02 (Online Advanced Phase):** Advanced simulation challenges & technical video submission. Dates TBA.
  * **Phase 03 (In-Person Finale):** Held at University of Moratuwa with provided physical robots.
* **The Final Format & Hardware:**
  * School finalists do **not** need to build hardware from scratch.
  * A custom RoboGames physical line-following robot is mailed directly to qualifying teams.
  * Teams must construct an exact replica of the arena in their own space based on dimensions in the final task booklet.
  * Teams develop and flash their control algorithms onto the mailed physical robot, tuning it for stability before competing at the live arena.
* **Technical Guidance & FAQs:**
  * **Webots Physics Step Warning:** Reduce the `basicTimeStep` parameter under `WorldInfo` in the Scene Tree to a value between **8 and 16**. Submissions are benchmarked with the same `basicTimeStep`, ensuring fair execution time.
  * **Video Submission:** Mandatory to confirm robot consistency between participant local environments and evaluation servers. Created using the Webots built-in recording tool or screen recording tools like OBS Studio.

---

### 2.2 University Category
*Advanced autonomous robotics, algorithmic pathfinding, strategy, and optimization.*

* **Target Participants:** Undergraduates from state universities and UGC-approved private universities in Sri Lanka.
* **Team Structure:** Teams of 2–5 members from the same institution.
* **Competition Path:**
  * **Phase 01 (Online Qualifier):** Webots algorithm submission. Dates TBA.
  * **Phase 02 (Online Bracket):** Advanced simulation evaluation & head-to-head bracket. Dates TBA.
  * **Phase 03 (In-Person Finale):** University arena final held at the University of Moratuwa.
* **Important Guidelines:**
  * All code must strictly follow official technical booklets, interfaces, and permitted library dependencies.
  * Judges reserve the right to conduct code reviews or a viva session for validation.
  * Teams must validate edge cases, recovery behaviors, route planning, and timing constraints under permitted arena conditions.

---

### 2.3 Open / BattleBots Category
*Full-contact physical combat robotics in a polycarbonate-enclosed arena.*

* **Target Participants:** Open to anyone — no restrictions on age or institutional affiliation. Independent builders, industry teams, university/school students, and international competitors are welcome.
* **Team Structure:** Individual entries or teams of up to 5 members.
* **Tournament Route:**
  * **Phase 01 (Entry):** Online registration & technical declaration. Dates TBA.
  * **Phase 02 (On-Site):** Mandatory weight, electrical, mechanical, and safety inspection at University of Moratuwa.
  * **Phase 03 (Grand Finale):** Single-elimination knockout tournament inside the protected arena.
* **Safety & Inspection Constraints:**
  * **No robot enters the arena without passing the safety inspection.**
  * Strict weight classes and dimensional limitations enforced.
  * Must feature fail-safe radio control, emergency shutoff switches, and secure battery/weapon containment.
  * Repairs are restricted to allocated maintenance windows; major modifications post-inspection are strictly prohibited.

---

## 3. General Rules & Regulations

1. **Category Exclusivity:** A participant may only compete in **one** category per competition year.
2. **Team Exclusivity:** An individual may register and compete in **only one group**.
3. **Institutional Integrity:** All team members in School and University categories must belong to the same eligible school or university.
4. **Anti-Plagiarism & Fair Play:** Strict zero-tolerance policy. Copied code, reused videos, or dishonest declarations lead to immediate disqualification.
5. **Workshops:** Preparatory workshops and awareness sessions are conducted by CSE / IESL for School and University participants.
